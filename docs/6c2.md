## 2. Dark Mode Architecture

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The dark mode was deliberately designed to avoid harsh, cold developer themes (`#000000` / high-contrast neon). Instead, it embodies a **peaceful, spiritual sanctuary**:
- **Background:** Deep obsidian slate-forest tone (`hsl(160 20% 7%)` / `#0f1412`).
- **Card Surfaces:** Serene slate-green surface with subtle borders (`hsl(160 20% 10%)` / `#141c19`).
- **Typography:** Warm alabaster/soft cream for primary text (`hsl(34 20% 90%)`) and soft sage-gray for secondary text (`hsl(160 10% 75%)`).
- **Accents:** Restrained midnight gold (`hsl(38 45% 25%)` with foreground `hsl(38 65% 82%)`) for sacred highlights.
- **Backdrop Atmosphere:** Ambient auroras (`bg-primary/5` and `bg-accent/10`) provide a soft, glowing twilight gradient.

### Tokens in `app/globals.css`
```css
.dark {
  --background: 160 20% 7%;      /* #0f1412 - deep spiritual obsidian */
  --foreground: 34 20% 90%;      /* soft warm cream text */
  --card: 160 20% 10%;           /* #141c19 - slate green card */
  --card-foreground: 34 20% 90%;
  --popover: 160 20% 10%;
  --popover-foreground: 34 20% 90%;
  --primary: 158 35% 42%;        /* active tranquil sage green */
  --primary-foreground: 160 20% 7%;
  --secondary: 160 18% 14%;      /* darker forest green surface */
  --secondary-foreground: 34 20% 90%;
  --muted: 160 10% 45%;
  --muted-foreground: 160 10% 75%;/* serene sage-gray text */
  --accent: 38 45% 25%;          /* soft midnight gold */
  --accent-foreground: 38 65% 82%;
  --border: 160 15% 16%;
  --input: 160 15% 16%;
  --ring: 158 35% 42%;
}
```

## 3. Theme Toggle & Header Integration

### Implementation (`components/ThemeToggle.jsx`)
- Leverages existing `next-themes` (`useTheme`) without third-party bloat.
- **Hydration Safe:** Uses client-mounted state guard to prevent SSR markup mismatches, rendering a dimensionally identical placeholder during pre-hydration.

  - Visible focus indicators (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-2`).
  - Screen reader auxiliary text (`<span className="sr-only">`).
- **Placement:** Positioned in `DashboardHeader` alongside the user profile badge and logout button, matching the `46px` height and rounded geometry. Also unified in `components/layout/Navbar.jsx`.

### 1. Two Distinct Visual Identities & Architectural Separation
Phase 6C.2.4 establishes strict visual separation between two dedicated domains within the application:

1. **Main Sanctuary Dashboard (`/dashboard`):**
   - **Theme:** Mental Wellness & Emotional Mindfulness.
   - **Concept:** "Calm Breath" (Pause → Breathe → Reset).
   - **Visual Language:** Completely abstract, modern, and calming. No religious imagery, no deities, no Krishna, no flute, no peacock feather, and no Vrindavan forest motifs.
   - **Palette:** Soft sage green, warm cream, faint serene blue (Light) | Spiritual obsidian slate, deep forest, muted sage (Dark).
2. **Chaitanyam AI Companion (`/dashboard/chaitanyam`):**
   - **Theme:** Krishna-Inspired Wisdom Companion.
   - **Visual Language:** Sacred lotus, peacock feather (mayura-pankh) motifs, Krishna flute (bansuri) line art, Vrindavan forest atmosphere, starlight/golden ambient particles.
   - **Palette:** Peacock blue, Vrindavan forest green, restrained sacred gold, and deep slate obsidian.

### 2. Provided Assets & Integration
Two official visual assets were integrated into the Chaitanyam AI experience:
- **`public/chaitanyam-assets/chaitanyam-ai-icon.png` (101 KB):**
  - High-resolution circular emblem featuring a glowing cream/gold lotus, water ripple base, and golden starlight sparkles on a deep forest-emerald background.
  - Used consistently across:
    - Chaitanyam Page Header (`components/krishna/ChaitanyamWorkspace.jsx`)
    - Chaitanyam Drawer Header (`components/krishna/ChaitanyamHeader.jsx`)
    - Chaitanyam Floating Launcher Trigger (`components/krishna/ChaitanyamTrigger.jsx`)
    - Chaitanyam Empty/Welcome State (`components/krishna/ChaitanyamMessageList.jsx`)
    - Chaitanyam Assistant Message Avatar (`components/krishna/ChaitanyamMessageList.jsx`)
    - Dashboard Entry Feature Card (`components/krishna/ChaitanyamCard.jsx`)
  - Aspect ratio and transparency preserved (`object-contain p-0.5`).
- **`public/chaitanyam-assets/chaitanyam-bg-light.png` (121 KB):**
  - Atmospheric artwork with soft cream/off-white gradient on the left/center and Krishna's golden flute with peacock feathers and pearls on the right.
  - Used **strictly and exclusively** on `/dashboard/chaitanyam` as an atmospheric background layer.
  - Never rendered at full harsh strength directly behind chat text; layered underneath soft cream/forest contrast veils and gradients to guarantee 100% text legibility.

### 3. Light-Mode Implementation (`/dashboard/chaitanyam`)
Layered CSS background architecture in `components/krishna/ChaitanyamAtmosphere.jsx`:
1. Warm cream base: `bg-[#fbf9f4]`.
2. Artwork layer: `bg-no-repeat bg-right bg-cover md:bg-contain opacity-70` showcasing the flute and peacock feather on the right.
3. Contrast gradients: `bg-gradient-to-r from-[#fbf9f4]/95 via-[#fbf9f4]/80 to-transparent` protecting chat bubbles, citations, and the composer.
4. Soft radial auroras: Peacock blue (`sky-500/10`) and warm gold (`amber-400/12`).

### 4. Dark-Mode Implementation (`/dashboard/chaitanyam`)
Because only a light asset was supplied, dark mode was engineered via sophisticated CSS layering rather than an artificial darkening:
1. Deep obsidian forest foundation: `bg-[#0b120f]`.
2. Blended artwork layer: `dark:opacity-15 dark:mix-blend-luminosity dark:contrast-125 dark:brightness-90`, creating moonlight illumination.
3. Ambient glows:
   - Peacock-blue radial glow (`dark:bg-teal-500/15 blur-[120px]`).
   - Restrained gold accent glow (`dark:bg-amber-500/10 blur-[110px]`).
   - Vrindavan deep forest mist (`dark:bg-emerald-950/30 blur-[130px]`).
4. Result: The environment feels like an intentional, sacred twilight forest transitioning naturally from the light mode.

### 5. Peacock Feather Treatment (`components/krishna/PeacockFeatherMotif.jsx`)
- Lightweight vector SVG representing the sacred Mayura-pankh with quill shaft, lateral barbs, and concentric ocellus eye (peacock blue `#0284c7`, emerald green `#059669`, cyan glint `#38bdf8`, and golden rim `#f59e0b`).
- Set to `opacity-20` (light) / `opacity-30` (dark).
- Applied as a soft atmospheric watermark in the upper-right corner of the workspace.
- Features subtle breathing keyframe animation (14s cycle) which is completely neutralized when `prefers-reduced-motion` is detected.
- `aria-hidden="true"`, `pointer-events-none`.

### 6. Flute Treatment (`components/krishna/FluteMotif.jsx`)
- Lightweight vector SVG line art of Krishna's bansuri flute with golden bamboo body (`#d97706` to `#fbbf24`), embouchure blow hole, 6 sacred tone holes, colored thread bindings, and hanging pearl/bead tassels.
- Positioned as a subtle watermark in the lower-right atmosphere on desktop viewports (`lg:block`).
- Low opacity (`opacity-15` to `opacity-20`), `aria-hidden="true"`, `pointer-events-none`.

### 7. Vrindavan Forest Atmosphere
- Ambient starlight and golden pollen drift simulated using 3 particles via `AmbientParticles` (`variant="dots"`, `color="accent"`, 16s cycle).
- Slow radial mist breathing cycle (`duration={18}`).
- Chat surfaces retain clean elevated cards (`bg-card/95 backdrop-blur-xs`) ensuring citations and text remain the visual focal point.

### 8. Dashboard Mental-Wellness Animation (`components/dashboard/layout/CalmBreathAtmosphere.jsx`)
- Replaced previous static background blur divs in `app/dashboard/page.js` with `CalmBreathAtmosphere`.
- **"Calm Breath" Visual Mechanics:**
  - Primary breathing orb with an 18-second slow expansion (`scale(1.06)`) and contraction (`scale(0.96)`) cycle.
  - Secondary tranquil aura drifting gently in opposing corner (22-second cycle).
  - 3 minimal floating dots in the quiet backdrop (`AmbientParticles count={3} variant="dots" color="secondary"`).
  - Palette strictly aligned with mental wellness: sage green, soft cream, muted lavender/sky in light mode; obsidian slate, deep forest, and indigo slate in dark mode.

### 9. Why Main Dashboard Does NOT Use Chaitanyam Assets
The main dashboard serves as a general mental-wellness and daily mindfulness hub for users of all backgrounds. Introducing religious deity imagery, flutes, or Vrindavan forest assets onto the dashboard background would dilute its universal mindfulness mission. Thus, the Krishna-inspired assets are exclusively reserved for the Chaitanyam AI workspace (`/dashboard/chaitanyam`) and its designated feature entry card (`ChaitanyamCard.jsx`).

### 10. Animation Architecture & Reduced-Motion Behavior
- All animations use CSS `transform` and `opacity` properties on GPU-accelerated layers (`will-change-transform`).
- Zero reliance on JavaScript render loops or canvas/WebGL contexts.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` compliance across:
  - `PeacockFeatherMotif`: animation disabled via CSS `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` and `useReducedMotion()`.
  - `FluteMotif`: shimmer disabled.
  - `ChaitanyamAtmosphere`: particles hidden automatically by `AmbientParticles`.
  - `CalmBreathAtmosphere`: breathing orb and aura frozen into serene static gradient lighting.

### 11. Performance Decisions
- **Zero Heavy Runtimes:** No Three.js, WebGL, canvas, video backgrounds, or GIF animations.
- **Battery-Friendly:** Staggered keyframes with 14s–22s cycle durations; particle counts strictly capped at 3 items.
- **Mobile Responsive:** Decorative watermarks hide on narrow mobile viewports (`hidden lg:block` for the wide flute watermark); background images scale adaptively (`bg-cover md:bg-contain`).
